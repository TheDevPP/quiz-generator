# User Interview — Quiz Generator (Health Quiz)

- **Who:** Close friend, university student, age 20
- **When:** 2026-07-16
- **How:** In person, ~15 minutes, tested on mobile phone

## What they do today (without your project)

They search Google or ask friends when they have health questions. Sometimes they find information in English that is hard to understand, or they find conflicting answers on different websites. They avoid asking doctors directly because it feels embarrassing for topics like reproductive health. They have never used a dedicated health quiz app before.

## What they liked

- "The privacy notice at the bottom made me feel safe — I liked that it says you collect nothing."
- The quiz felt friendly and not like a school exam — the emoji icons on the category buttons made it feel approachable.
- The explanation after each answer was the most useful part — "I learned something even when I got it right."
- The True or False questions were their favorite format — "It's fast and I find out if I believed something wrong."
- The app loaded quickly and worked well on their phone.

## What confused them / what's missing

- When they first opened the app, they were not sure if the categories were buttons — they looked like labels at first. They hesitated before clicking.
- After answering all questions in a category, the "You've answered all questions" message appeared but they were not sure if they should pick the same category again or a different one — the message was not clear enough.
- They wanted to see their score at the end — "How many did I get right out of all the questions?"
- There is no way to go back and review the questions they got wrong after finishing.
- They asked: "Are these questions written by a real doctor?" — the "Doctor reviewed ✓" badge was small and they almost missed it.
- No Burmese language option — "My younger sibling would use this but they can't read English well."

## What would make them actually use it

- A score summary at the end of each category showing correct / total
- Burmese language support so they can share it with younger family members
- More questions — they ran out quickly and felt the quiz was too short
- Make the category buttons look more obviously clickable (add an arrow or "Start" label)
- Make the "Doctor reviewed" badge more visible so users trust the content immediately

## What I'll change (next steps)

- [ ] Add a score counter visible during the quiz (e.g. "3 correct out of 5")
- [ ] Add a session summary screen after all questions are completed showing final score and a "Try another category" button
- [ ] Make category buttons more obviously interactive (add a → arrow or subtle animation on load)
- [ ] Make the "Doctor reviewed ✓" badge larger and more prominent on the question card
- [ ] Add more questions per category (minimum 10 per category for a satisfying session)
- [ ] Plan Burmese language as the next major feature after the admin UI is complete
